### How I work

**I start with the big picture.** Before writing any code, I look at the market, the users and what already exists, to land on the right specs and a long-term vision for the product.

**Then I break it down.** Vision → phases → milestones → issues. Each issue stays open to rework as the team learns, so the plan moves with the project instead of against it.

**I care about the people building it.** I love working in a team and helping everyone find where they do their best work. Scrum keeps the rhythm: short sprints, clear priorities, regular reviews.

**And I care about how it looks.** I have a real eye for design and detail. A product should work well *and* feel good to use, and that's why I enjoy building design systems.
